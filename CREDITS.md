# Credits

Cathedral of Tristram is a fan-made, non-commercial browser homage to Blizzard's Diablo (1996). It ships **no Blizzard files**: no MPQ data, sprites, sounds, music or text taken from the game.

## Art and audio
- Order 1 uses code-drawn placeholder shapes only (no image or audio files).
- CC0 art from the Order 2 art pack will be listed here, one line per file with author, source URL and license.

## Game data references (numbers only, re-typed by hand)
- Jarulf's Guide to Diablo and Hellfire v1.62 (Pedro Faria "Jarulf"): life/mana, to-hit, damage and experience formulas.
- diasurgical/devilution (github.com/diasurgical/devilution): reconstructed v1.09 tables (player.cpp, monstdat.cpp, itemdat.cpp, spelldat.cpp), used to check values.
